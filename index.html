We strictly use ISI-certified flame-retardant (FR) copper wires from trusted brands like Havells, Polycab, and Finolex. Every circuit is routed through heavy-duty PVC conduit pipes inside walls with dedicated earthing and RCCB leakage circuit breakers to prevent shocks and short circuits.

For wet areas like modular kitchens, we exclusively use Boiling Water Proof (BWP / BWR 710) marine plywood resistant to moisture, steam, and termites. For wardrobes, we use commercial MR/BWP calibrated plywood with acrylic or soft-touch laminate shutters and German Blum/Hettich hardware.
