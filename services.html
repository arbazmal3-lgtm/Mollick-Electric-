Craftsmanship & Engineering

Our Complete Suite of Services

Executed by licensed technicians and master carpenters under the personal supervision of Najmul Mollick.

1. Concealed Electric Work & Automation

Full 1-phase & 3-phase residential/commercial wiring. Heavy-duty conduit wall channeling, MCB/RCCB distribution boards, inverter battery setups, copper earthing, and smart touch automation switches.

  • Havells / Polycab flame-retardant copper cables
  • Zero-shock RCCB leakage trip safety
  • Smart Wi-Fi touch switchboard compatibility

2. Designer False Ceiling & Cove Lights

Architectural gypsum false ceilings for living rooms, bedrooms, and dining halls. Features seamless cove lighting, magnetic track spotlights, sound insulation, and zero-crack GI framing.

  • Gyproc Saint-Gobain moisture-resistant boards
  • 3000K warm indirect ambient LED troughing
  • Customized geometric or organic chandelier drops

3. Luxury Acrylic & PU Modular Kitchen

Engineered for Indian cooking conditions. Heavy-duty 100% boiling water-proof (BWP 710) marine ply carcass, German Blum/Hettich soft-close tandem boxes, quartz countertops, and Gola profiles.

  • Termite & boiling water proof marine carcass
  • High-gloss scratch-resistant acrylic shutters
  • Under-cabinet LED task sensor lighting

4. Modern TV Wall Units & Louvers

Bespoke entertainment walls featuring Italian marble sheets or nano-white stone, acoustic fluted charcoal/teak louvers, concealed wiring channels, and floating soundbar media shelves.

  • 100% invisible wiring & power sockets
  • Acoustic sound-dampening fluted paneling
  • Warm LED aura backlighting

5. Floor-to-Ceiling Sliding Wardrobes

Space-maximizing customized wardrobes featuring smooth heavy-gauge aluminum sliding tracks, tinted fluted glass doors, motion-sensor LED clothing rails, and luxury velvet organizer drawers.

  • Smooth anti-jump roller sliding mechanisms
  • Automatic door-open warm LED illumination
  • Custom trouser pullouts & jewelry vanity

6. Sacred Backlit CNC Jaali Pooja Mandir

Peaceful temple sanctuaries custom made with CNC laser cut backlit jaali, teakwood and corian carvings, hanging brass bells, pullout diya trays, and storage for holy samagri.

  • Spiritual OM & floral CNC backlit pattern
  • Smoke-resistant brass and polish protection
  • Dedicated pullout aarti and diya tray

7. CCTV Surveillance & Smart Security

4K color night vision security cameras, IP cameras, 8-channel NVR, encrypted remote smartphone view, video door phones, and Wi-Fi access point cabling.

  • Crystal-clear 24/7 color night vision
  • Live monitoring on iOS and Android devices
  • Concealed weatherproof cabling

8. Italian Marble & Large Slab Tiles

Precision laser-guided floor laying of 4x2 / 6x4 vitrified tiles, Italian marble, and granite. Includes waterproof epoxy joint sealing and mirror crystallization buffing.

  • Zero-lippage laser leveled alignment
  • Epoxy stain-resistant waterproof grout
  • Diamond floor mirror crystallization

9. Luxury Emulsion Paint, Putty & Wallpapers

Silky 3-coat machine-sanded Birla White putty, primer sealer, and Asian Paints Royale luxury emulsion. Includes Italian stucco textures, stencil accent walls, and imported non-woven wallpapers.

  • Zero-dust mechanical orbital sanding
  • Washable, stain-resistant luxury paints
  • Imported non-woven 3D designer wallpapers

10. Designer Storage Beds & Headboards

Bespoke hydraulic lift-up king and queen storage beds. Includes cushioned velvet/leatherette acoustic headboards, integrated dual nightstands with USB charging ports, and solid hardwood framing.

  • Heavy-duty hydraulic lift cylinders
  • Plush high-density cushioned headboard
  • Dual nightstands with built-in switchboards
