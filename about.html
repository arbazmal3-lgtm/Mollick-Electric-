Who We Are

Where Electrical Precision Meets Bespoke Interior Design

Mollick Electric & Interior was founded with a straightforward mission: eliminate the dangerous compromises commonly made in residential electrical wiring and interior woodwork.

Too often, homeowners face disconnected contractors — an electrician who damages the false ceiling, or carpenters who bury dangerous electrical wires inside wooden panels with no safety conduits. We bridge this gap by offering a cohesive, unified team led directly by licensed master technician Najmul Mollick.

Whether we are rewiring a heritage property with modern MCBs and fire-retardant Havells cables or building a sleek, acrylic modular kitchen with German soft-close hardware, every screw, wire, and panel is installed to perfection.

🛡️ Safety-First Engineering

Zero compromises on cable thickness, earthing pits, and circuit load balancing.

⭐ Pure Material Transparency

Authentic ISI copper, genuine BWP 710 marine plywood, and original branded paints.

⏱️ Guaranteed Timelines

Committed schedules backed by dedicated in-house craftsmen and daily updates.
